04 — Platform

The intelligence layer above your hardware

SecureIQ ingests events from VSS, access control, intrusion and ISMS, learns what normal looks like for each zone, and surfaces the correlated threats that rule-based systems miss. No rip-and-replace — it sits on top of the systems you already run.

  • Unsupervised detection. Trained on normal behaviour only, so no labelled attack data is required.
  • Correlated events. Access, video and intrusion signals scored together at zone level.
  • Operational control. Live threshold tuning, experiment tracking and model registry in one place.

05 — Approach

From survey to steady state

One team owns the design, installation and operation — so accountability does not disappear between trades, vendors or handover documents.

01 / ASSESS

Site survey

Walk the facility, audit existing systems and map risk, coverage, cable routes and constraints before a single line is drawn.

02 / DESIGN

Coordinated design

03 / DEPLOY

Build & commission

04 / OPERATE
